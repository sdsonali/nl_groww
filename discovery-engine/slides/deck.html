03 · USER RESEARCH

Five interviews. No survey was fielded.

HYPOTHESIS

If we name the cause of a missed instalment and offer one safe next step, more of those SIPs are completed before the next due date. If the money has already left, the step is an explanation, not a second payment.

BEHAVIOUR

New investors leave when the first months are hard to read. A debit that vanishes creates a support visit. Some redeem at the first visible gain. Some almost never look.

OPPORTUNITY

Explain the debit inside the app, before they search. Do not take the neighbouring jobs yet: redemption tracking, return alerts, or “should I cancel.”

05 · THE TRUE PROBLEM

The month fails in the gap between the bank and a screen they cannot read

ROOT CAUSE

The app treats “payment failed”, “money already left”, and “I cannot read this” as the same dead end. People then search, pay twice, or cancel.

HOW WE KNOW

  • It is still the largest in-scope tag after questions were removed.
  • Three misses can be treated as a discontinued SIP.
  • Madhuri lived the missing debit. Akshay, 22, lived the unreadable screen.
  • We do not know the Groww rate. Seventeen comments cannot carry one.

THE JOB, IN ONE LINE

When this month’s instalment does not land, help me see what happened and finish it, or tell me not to pay again.

WHO

A first-time mutual-fund SIP investor. Small amount. New words: NAV, mandate, allotment. One confusing month is enough.

WHY NOW

FY26 discontinuations sit close to new SIPs. Groww opens a large share of those new SIPs. Waiting for a perfect public rate delays a screen we can test inside.

VALUE FOR THE PERSON

One month completed, or a clear “do not pay again.” Less time in support. No advice, and no fee for the repair.

VALUE FOR THE BUSINESS

A better chance the SIP is still running at month 12. Trust at the moment people currently leave. No claim on the ₹46,624 crore of FY26 SIP inflows.

Not a forecast. Not investment advice.

MVP, IN ONE BREATH

Sample SIP, ₹2,000 on the 5th, bank ending 4821. Four causes. Three short steps. One green action. Chat opens with “Hi, what can I help you with?” If the answer is not enough, offer a call. This demo cannot place it, and it cannot move money.

GUESTIMATE, LABELED

If 5% of 10.03 million miss one instalment, and if 40% of those are still open at the next due date, that is about 2.0 lakh instalments, about ₹40 crore at ₹2,000. Both rates are assumptions. Do not use ₹40 crore without them. It is not a measured loss.

Priority is for this leak only. The prototype already contains the first four rows.

08 · RISK, DISTRIBUTION, MONEY

Do not charge for the repair. Do not pay the month twice.

BUSINESS RISK · HIGH IMPACT

A second pay button after the money has left creates the next complaint. Mitigation: that path has no pay button. This screen takes no fee. A recovered ₹2,000 is retained contribution, not a new charge.

MARKET RISK · MEDIUM

Recurring-debit failure is industry-wide. The screen can be copied. Mitigation: show it on our own failed debit, with our own next date, not as a separate app or an ad.

OPERATIONAL RISK · MEDIUM

A bot that guesses increases support. That is Madhuri’s experience. Mitigation: under 0.6 confidence, refuse, then offer a call. The support link is still to be verified. The demo cannot place the call.

HOW THE PROTOTYPE IS BUILT

Screen

A state machine. The four causes and the one green button do not call a model.

Chat

Masks personal numbers. Refuses advice, news, returns and fund names. At most two model calls. Replies follow the language of the message. The visit stays in the browser.

Not yet true

The rate is not instrumented. The chat score has not been run. The prototype shows the path. It does not report the north star.

Decision

Measure the internal unrecovered rate. Keep this scope. Replace the assumption guestimate only after that rate exists.
