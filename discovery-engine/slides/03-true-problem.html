12. PROBLEM FRAMING

This is not “they chose to leave.” The instalment fails, or the person cannot tell what happened, and the page does not separate a bank change, a failed debit, and money that has already left. They then search, pay by hand, or pay twice. Three misses can be counted as a stop.

THE JOBWhen this month’s instalment does not land, help me see what happened and finish it, or tell me not to pay again. If the bank changed, let me update it on the page.
