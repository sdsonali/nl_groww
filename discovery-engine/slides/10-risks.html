RISKS AND MITIGATIONS

Why it might fail

  • A second debit. If money has already left and the screen still offers pay, Madhuri is charged again. That is the failure the page exists to stop.
  • A mark, not an allotment. The demo is not connected to the bank or the fund house. “Month received” does not mean units were allotted.
  • The wrong base. Groww is 17 of the 333 comments. A screen built only on Groww’s own reviews would have missed the leak. 39.8% is comments, not a customer rate.
  • They never open it. The old path is a search outside the app. If the page is not on the failed instalment, the four chips do not get used.

What we would change

  • Do not ship a pay button on the money-left path. A second debit on that path stays at zero.
  • Say it on the screen. Confirming does not move money. Live debit and allotment stay a later project.
  • Keep the full file. Do not read success from 39.8%, and do not multiply it by 10.03 million users.
  • Put it on the failed SIP. If people ask for support before the helper answers, review the chips. The helper tries first, then Help in the Groww app.
  • A reminder, if added, names ₹2,000, 5 December, and the account ending 4821. It does not read the bank balance, and it does not say pay now when the money has already left. The bank’s own notice, at least 24 hours before a UPI Autopay debit, still has to go out. NPCI.
