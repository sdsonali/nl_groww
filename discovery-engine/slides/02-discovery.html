SEARCH ENGINE
WHY THIS ENGINE

The question is: of people who start a mutual-fund SIP, how many still have one running 12 months later? Active means one successful instalment in the last 30 days. Public comments were collected and sorted onto that path, so a failed month could be seen before the interviews and the prototype. It is a reading aid. It is not a customer rate, and it does not recommend a fund.

What the file found

333 of 837 (39.8%) are a failed debit, expired mandate, bank change, or short balance. 289 (86.8%) still continuing. 3 (0.9%) ended. Groww is 17, a pattern, not a rate.

Metric tree and ranking

  • The tree is the 12-month path. A1 and A2 are setup and the first debit. B is months 1–3. C1 is a chosen stop. C2 is the failed debit. C3 is a miss fixed in time. D1 is a successful instalment in the last 30 days. E1 is a restart. None is everything else.
  • Each box gets a count. The largest in-scope leak is outlined and labelled Largest leak. A bar under the boxes shows the same counts. On this file that box is C2. The chart highlights that box. The app menu opens on Groww. A count under 30 is marked too few to rely on.
  • Ranking is how common × how serious × how fixable. Fixability is set by hand, not by the model. Severity stays 1 when the SIP is still continuing, including a failed debit the comment does not say ended. That is why C2 can lead even though 86.8% of those comments are still open.

Questions: how to ask

Type one question and press Find the answer. No list. The answer is a short lead, sometimes a table, and quotes from the comments. Apps: Groww, other apps, YouTube, or all. Under 30 says too few. Unmatched questions search the comment text. “Why do people pause a SIP?” fits. Income, age, and influence are mostly unstated.

Try one comment, and what stays out

Paste a public comment and press Tag this comment. The page returns the box, a confidence from 0 to 1, and a quote copied from the paste. Under 0.60, or if a check fails, it says Not sure, needs human review. That paste is not saved and is not added to the ranking. 188 comments are already in that unsure set. They stay out of the tree and the ranking. Themes are confusion points, unmet needs, workarounds outside the app, and chosen stops against failed debits.

How the tags were checked

105 comments were labelled by hand, 15 in each of 7 groups. The first check agreed on the stage for 47 of 105. Six misses were written into the prompt: a question, a hypothetical, a wish to start, or money that has not come back after a cancel is not that person’s failed or cancelled SIP. The odd 53 rows tuned the prompt. The even 52 were the test, once, at temperature 0: stage 44 of 52 (was 24), exact tag 40 of 52 (was 21). Stage C fell from 14 of 14 to 10 of 14. The full file was then retagged. Frozen 2 November 2026. NVIDIA, one comment per call. Not a survey.
