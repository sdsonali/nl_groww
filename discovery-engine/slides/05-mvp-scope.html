MVP SCOPE AND FEATURE PRIORITIZATION

MVP SCOPE

One screen for one monthly SIP when this month did not finish. The demo SIP is ₹2,000, due on the 5th, from a bank ending 4821, open month November. Confirming does not move money. Nothing is written back to the comment file.

In scope: C2, a SIP already running. Out: first setup (A1 15.3%, A2 8.8%) and a chosen stop (C1 29.4%).

THE MVP FOCUSES ON

  • Which break. Four chips: bank rejected the payment, autopay didn’t send it, money left the bank but is not in the SIP, bank change.
  • One next step. Pay this month only, update the bank, wait for units, or renew autopay. Three steps on the screen.
  • Helper, then a person. The chat sits on the same page. Support is offered after an answer.
  • A safe demo. No second debit. No fund name. No real payment. No real support agent.

VALUE PROPOSITION

  • For the person. The cause is named. Kritika can put the new bank on the page. Madhuri is told not to pay again. This month can still be allotted.
  • For the business. A finished month can be counted before three misses. C3, fixed in time, is 4 of 837 (0.5%). No fee. No claim on ₹46,624 crore of FY26 SIP inflows.
  • For trust. The helper does not name a fund, predict a return, or show an account number.

ATOMIC UNIT

One loop: name the break, take the one safe step, close this month. The amount stays ₹2,000. The next date stays 5 December. Not a new SIP. Not a fund choice.

RISKIEST HYPOTHESIS

People finish the month on this page once the cause is named and the next step cannot charge them twice. If they still search outside the app, or pay by hand, or pay again, the four chips did not fix the dead end.

Not measured. No survey was fielded. 86.8% of the 333 C2 comments say the SIP is still continuing, so the person is still in the month. That is not a completion rate.

WHAT THIS DEMO ALREADY RUNS

The screen was used on that sample SIP. Each chip walks a 3-step path. The bank form accepts an optional account-holder name. Bank, account, phone, and address stay ***. The four chips change the screen with no model call. A page with no model would still show those chips. It would not answer a typed question, and it would not decline a fund. A written question uses NVIDIA only, temperature 0, at most two calls. If it is unsure, it says so instead of guessing. Satisfaction is Yes, or No, connect me with support. History is this visit only. The handoff is a demo ticket that no person receives.

GTM STRATEGY

  • Where. On the SIP that already failed, inside the app. Groww, FY26, opened over 43% of new SIPs. Not an ad. Not a new fund.
  • Who. A running SIP. Kritika first, Madhuri second. Not someone setting up, and not someone deciding to stop.
  • Message. Name what happened. Offer one safe step, or say do not pay again.
  • Moment. Debit failed, autopay did not send, money already left, or the bank changed. Before the next 5th, and before three misses.
  • Learn. Did this month finish here, or was a second payment refused? The 39.8% is comments. The ₹40 crore line is an assumption (5% miss, 40% still open), not a target.

MVP EXPECTATIONS

  • The chip or the chat opens the matching screen.
  • A bank change ends on the form. The account number stays masked.
  • Money already left has no pay button.
  • Support comes after an answer. The demo says nobody receives the ticket.
  • Pilot bar, not a result: the month is finished before the next debit date. The file records that fix 0.5% of the time today.
