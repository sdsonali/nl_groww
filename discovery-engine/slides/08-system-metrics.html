PRE-LAUNCH GUARDRAILS

No second debit. Money already left has no pay button. If a test shows one, do not ship. Madhuri’s month would be charged again.

No advice. The helper names no fund and no return. If it does, do not ship. The account number stays ***.

No real money and no real agent. Confirming does not move money. The handoff is a demo ticket nobody receives. A live debit with the bank and fund house is a later project.
