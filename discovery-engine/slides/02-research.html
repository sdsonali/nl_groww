INVOLUNTARY LEAKS: FAILED DEBIT, EXPIRED MANDATE, BANK CHANGE, LOW BALANCE
HYPOTHESIS
  • This is not a chosen stop. The month breaks in one of four ways: the debit fails, the mandate expires, the bank changes, or the balance is short. Those four are the largest in-scope group, 333 of 837 comments (39.8%). 86.8% of them say the SIP is still continuing. Only 0.9% say it ended. People are stuck in the month.
  • The four are not one action. A bank change needs a form on the page, which Kritika, 30, never got. Money that has already left needs a wait or a return, not a second debit. That is Madhuri, 26. A failed debit can still be paid once. An expired mandate is a renew for the next date, not a new SIP.
  • Today the page shows one dead end, so the person searches, pays by hand, or pays again. AMFI, quoting SEBI: three monthly misses in a row and that SIP can be treated as discontinued.
  • The bet: if the page names which of the four happened, and the next step cannot charge them twice, this month finishes before the next due date. Not measured. No survey was fielded. The same complaint is already public on the apps in the table. Groww’s 17 comments are a pattern, not a Groww rate.

Opportunities

Update the bank on the page

A form where the SIP already is, without waiting for an email, so this month can still be debited and allotted.

A bot on that same page

It answers first and reaches support when it cannot. Manas, 23, and Madhuri both asked for this. Return alerts and “should I stop?” stay out.

Behaviour

  • The app can be fine until the bank changes. Kritika got no form and no email, the instalment failed, and the NAV was not allotted.
  • A debit that does not appear becomes a support visit. That is Madhuri. Manas wants that help on the page he already has open.
  • Some pay by hand every month because autopay is not on the page until a payment is made. That is Ruchi.
