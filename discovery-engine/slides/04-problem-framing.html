PROBLEM PRIORITIZATION

WHAT IS THE TRUE PROBLEM?

C2 is a debit that failed, a mandate that expired, a bank change, or a balance that was short. On a SIP that is already running, that is the largest in-scope box: 333 of 837, 39.8%.

Why? No form when the bank changes.
Why? A failed debit and money that already left look the same.
Why? The next step is a search, a manual payment, or a second debit.

ROOT CAUSE

The page has one dead end for three different breaks. 86.8% of the 333 say the SIP is still continuing. Only 0.9% say it ended. People are stuck in the month.

WHY SOLVE IT NOW?

  • Competitive urgency. The same C2 complaint is public on other apps in this file: Zerodha Coin 122, ET Money 52, Paytm Money 21, Groww 17. Groww’s 17 are too few for a rate. The failure is already what people compare.
  • Market momentum. Monthly SIP book ₹25,926 cr in Mar 2025 to ₹32,087 cr in Mar 2026. In FY26, discontinued or completed SIPs were 94.5% of new SIPs. AMFI. That includes finished tenures.
  • Strategic alignment. Groww, FY26, opened over 43% of new SIPs and has 10.03 million mutual-fund users. This screen sits on the instalment Groww already opens.
  • Perfect timing. Three misses can be treated as a discontinued SIP. The fix-in-time box, C3, is 4 of 837 (0.5%). The leak is in the file. The recovery is not.

VALUE GENERATED

For the business. A failed month is recovered before three misses can count as a stop. The file almost never records that recovery: C3 is 0.5%. No fee on this screen. No claim on the ₹46,624 crore of FY26 SIP inflows.

For the user. They see what happened. The new bank goes on the page, or they are told not to pay again. This month can still be allotted. 86.8% of these comments say the SIP is still continuing, so the person is still here.
